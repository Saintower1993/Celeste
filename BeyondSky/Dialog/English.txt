PuzzleDial01=
In this map, you will see a certain numbers of Dream Blocks, and you need to dash through them under specific conditions and sequence.
There are two modes of the game: {#FF0064}Red Mode{#} and {#5FCDE4}Blue Mode{#}. When you dash through a Dream Block in {#FF0064}Red Mode{#}, you will turn it into Lava.
To win the game, you must turn all the 15 Dream Blocks into Lava.

PuzzleDial02=
In {#5FCDE4}Blue Mode{#}, you cannot turn Dream Blocks into Lava, and all the Lava you've already turned will be temporarily neutralized so you can safely go through them. 
However, in this mode, there will also be Ice Wall blocking your way.

PuzzleDial03=
When you enter the map, you will first be in Viewer Mode and cannot interact with most of the entities.
You need to pick one of the {#5FCDE4}Blue Switches{#} to turn on and start the game in {#5FCDE4}Blue Mode{#}. Remember, the location you pick is critical to solve the puzzle.

PuzzleDial04=
After the game starts, you can pick up keys to unlock {#FF0064}Red Switches{#} and change the mode to {#FF0064}Red Mode{#}.
Note that keys can only be picked up in {#5FCDE4}Blue Mode{#} and all the switches are for one-time use.

PuzzleDial05=
Due to this map containing a lot of triggers, it may start lagging after running for a while. If this happens, please quit the map and re-enter.
Thanks for playing!
